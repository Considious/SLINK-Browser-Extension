# SLINK — What changed and what to test

## What changed

- PDA's Market cleanup now shows imported Weaver price-list watches as well as individually saved price watches, including while access is locked. You can stop the imported watches or clear all Market watches from that screen. Clearing asks for confirmation and does not change your price list on Weaver.
- Switching Market price checks off also stops alerts from cached Market results.
- PDA's locked Market view now lists saved watches with a Remove button. Access has a Manage saved Market watches shortcut. Opening Market also reaches the permission-refresh step when no current grant is cached, rather than returning before checking newly assigned access.
- GIFs and stickers default to enabled when no preference has been saved. A saved off setting stays off, but the chat picker now offers an Enable button instead of an unexplained dead search box. Settings-load/save errors and Retry stay visible inside the picker; API-key settings remain in QOL > Chat.
- Market Watch stays visible when locked, with a working Refresh permissions button. Access also has a Refresh Market permissions button, so newly granted access can be picked up without hunting for a hidden tab.
- Museum cannot add Market watches without a current Market Watch permission. The same check applies to other watch-creation and activation paths, including Weaver selections.
- When Market access expires or is removed, saved watches stop checking prices and stop producing alerts from old results. Your watches are kept for when access returns.
- Rooms settings now lets you choose when to disconnect while unfocused: 1, 5, 15 or 60 minutes, or Never for inactivity (the default). Fixed background sign-in renewal being rejected merely because Torn was unfocused. Only one Torn tab monitors Rooms; Torn scraping still stops when unfocused.
- Devices using the HTTPS connection fallback check Rooms less frequently in the background and catch up when focus returns. Temporary sign-in outages retry with backoff rather than leaving monitoring permanently disconnected.
- Chat Leaders and room moderators can right-click or long-press a local member’s message to mute them or remove their room access. Choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, 12 hours, 1 day, 1 week or Until restored, then confirm the member, room and duration. Chat Leaders are protected; only a Leader can restrict another room moderator. These actions affect SLINK Rooms only.
- GIFs and stickers in Rooms now display automatically by default. Turn off **Show GIFs and stickers automatically** in Faction Rooms settings to use click-to-show instead.
- Temporary access duration can be chosen before selecting members, including bulk grants. Expiring access applies to selected-member rooms; All members access remains available to the whole faction.
- Leaving Torn’s focus no longer destroys the Rooms management form. Unfinished edits stay in place when you return, while background Torn collectors still stop.
- Rooms now has a secure connection fallback for devices such as iPhone PDA that reject its live connection. Members can still send, reply, edit and delete; mutes and room restrictions still apply.
- Room management now uses the Armory Recaller-style searchable checkbox list: find people by name, ID or faction role, then select or clear the shown members. Refresh members loads a missing faction directory instead of leaving search silently empty.
- Rooms can be open to All members, selected members or selected faction roles. Role access does not grant Chat Leader powers, and individual restrictions still apply.
- Affected PDA users can now explicitly choose **Delete unreadable records and rebuild** in Access → Storage troubleshooting. It deletes only SLINK settings records confirmed to have the native Null/String read failure, plus their associated pieces and pointers, then verifies a clean saved copy of the settings on the current page. Readable records, saved targets, Torn's browser storage and other scripts are left alone. This never runs automatically. Deleted records cannot be recovered.
- PDA room management now keeps its own mounted form through repeated unrelated module updates, preserving unfinished text.
- PDA's manual storage recovery now saves recovered data in smaller, verified pieces instead of another single growing record. Interrupted saves keep the last confirmed version. It can repair the reported failure recurring in the older recovery format, and no longer tries reading all old records as a fallback for recovered settings.
- War settings, War display-mode changes and individual module visibility changes no longer rebuild unrelated panels. Room management keeps its form and unfinished text. The Rooms loading label now clears after initialization.
- Fixed room management failing when the shared faction member-directory table was missing. Existing chat history, licenses and Leader assignments are unchanged. An empty member directory is not an online-user count.
- PDA users with the specific native Null/String settings-read failure can manually choose **Recover saving from this page** under Access → Storage troubleshooting. Recovery saves and verifies the settings currently available on the page before selecting a fresh native record. Original records remain untouched. It does not run automatically or change healthy users' saved storage.
- **Copy storage report** remains available in Access for investigating save failures without sharing your keys or saved settings.
- Torn's chat bar, unread badges and native popups now appear above Rooms instead of being covered by it. Room position, spacing and size are unchanged.
- Rooms now remembers being minimized even when sign-in or terms verification fails.
- Chat Central's updated configuration uses normal SLINK permissions and terms acceptance, preserving existing rooms and Leaders. This requires its Cloudflare build to deploy successfully; a failed deployment cannot be fixed by repeatedly accepting terms.
- Saved terms acceptance is rechecked after loading policy details, so accepting while a request is in progress does not leave it checking an older saved value.
- If services disagree, the error identifies that disagreement and preserves your saved acceptance, key and settings.

## What to test

- [ ] Open Access → Manage saved Market watches in PDA. Check individually saved and imported Weaver watches are listed separately, even without a Market tier. Stop the imported watches and check that source stops alerting.
- [ ] Try Clear all Market watches, then cancel: nothing should change. Confirm it next, reload and verify the saved list is cleared, imported watches are stopped, and Market stays off. Switching Market off must not leave cached price alerts firing. Do not use this test with watches you want to keep.
- [ ] In PDA, open Access → Manage saved Market watches. Remove a test watch, reload and confirm it stays removed. This must work without a Market tier; adding watches and price checks must remain locked. Assign access and reopen Market to check it picks up the new grant.
- [ ] Open the GIF/sticker picker inside Torn chat. Type a search, try GIFs and Stickers, close/reopen and try again. If media was switched off, Enable should save that choice and make search available. A settings failure should show a readable error and Retry, not a silently disabled box.
- [ ] With no Market Watch permission, confirm Market is visible but locked. Museum's Add Market watch control should be disabled. Existing watches must not check prices or produce alerts.
- [ ] Grant Market Watch access, then use Refresh permissions in Market or Refresh Market permissions in Access. Market controls should become usable immediately; adding a Museum watch should work.
- [ ] Remove or let Market Watch access expire while watches are saved. Confirm checks and alerts stop, then restore access and refresh permissions. Saved watches should still be there. Test the extension dashboard and PDA too.
- [ ] Leave Rooms monitoring while using another app for more than five minutes. With Never selected, check messages continue arriving. Choose a one-minute idle timeout, leave focus and verify it disconnects, then return and reconnect. Torn’s other background collectors should remain stopped.
- [ ] Open Rooms in two Torn tabs and switch between them. Only the most recently focused tab should monitor Rooms. Reload the extension’s background worker if you know how, then confirm the owner can still renew while unfocused.
- [ ] As a Chat Leader or room moderator, right-click/hold a member’s message, choose Mute and a duration, then cancel confirmation: nothing should change. Repeat and confirm; the member should still read but not send. Test timed removal in a test room and verify access returns after expiry on refresh. Restore any test restrictions afterward.
- [ ] Ordinary members must not see moderation actions or gain them by sending their own request. Chat Leaders must remain protected, and linked-faction messages must not offer local kick/mute actions.
- [ ] Send a GIF and sticker in Rooms. They should appear without pressing Show image. Switch automatic media off, reopen Rooms and verify previews require a click; reload and check the preference stays off.
- [ ] In a selected-member test room, choose a temporary duration before picking members. Grant access and check the displayed expiry. Change a selected member’s duration and verify it saves. Restore permissions after testing.
- [ ] Leave an unfinished room name or description in management, click another app or tab, then return. The form and draft should remain without a Loading saved state cycle.
- [ ] On the affected iPhone, open Rooms, send a message and confirm another member receives it. Reply, edit and delete your own message. Leave/reopen Rooms and check messages are not duplicated. The fallback can take a few seconds to receive new messages.
- [ ] In Manage rooms, search for a name, ID and faction role. Check the visible results match. Try Select shown/Clear shown in a restricted test room, and verify only those members change. Refresh members should populate an empty directory or display an actual error.
- [ ] Give a test room access to a faction role; check a member in that role can read and send, while an unassigned member cannot enter. Mute that member and verify they can read but cannot send. Restore their permissions afterward.
- [ ] For the recurring native Null/String settings error only: update the installed PDA script to 0.4.155, reload Torn and open Access → Storage troubleshooting. Choose **Delete unreadable records and rebuild** and carefully review the permanent-deletion confirmation. Keep the page open until success. Reload, navigate between pages and use SLINK for several minutes; check your theme, accepted terms and preferences remain saved. Cancelling must change nothing. If the option is hidden, Copy storage report checks whether this specific repair is applicable. Do not clear all storage.
- [ ] Leave room management open and type an unfinished room name. Change War preferences or its display mode from another SLINK window; confirm only War changes and the room form and draft remain. Hide/show another module and check that unrelated panels stay untouched too.
- [ ] Open Faction Rooms in the dashboard and check that room management loads without the HTTP 500 error. Check that existing rooms still open normally.
- [ ] If PDA has the native Null/String settings-read error, update the installed script to 0.4.154 or later and reload Torn. Open Access → Storage troubleshooting → Recover saving from this page. Review the confirmation. After success, reload, use several modules for a few minutes, then check your theme, terms acceptance and alert preferences still save. Settings missing from the current session are not automatically restored from unreadable originals. If recovery reports that its native allowance is full, follow that message rather than clearing data or repeatedly authenticating.
- [ ] If recovery fails or is unavailable, use Copy storage report and send the result with the error. Do not clear saved data.
- [ ] Receive a native Torn chat message while Rooms is open. Check that its unread badge is visible and clickable above Rooms.
- [ ] Open Torn chat settings or a native popup near Rooms. Check that Rooms does not cover it.
- [ ] Accept the current terms once in Access, then open Rooms and its management page. Confirm it does not repeatedly ask for the same acceptance.
- [ ] Minimize Rooms while it shows a sign-in or terms error, then change pages. Check that it stays minimized.
- [ ] Check existing rooms, message history and assigned Leaders still work after the chat service updates. A user with only administrator status must not gain room management unless explicitly assigned as a Chat Leader.
- [ ] Reload Torn, switch focus and resize chat. Confirm the corrected spacing and height still work.

Do not clear saved data to resolve a terms disagreement. Report the affected screen and error code instead.
